## 📌 About Project...........

SV Betel Farm Exports is a professional agricultural website developed to showcase premium quality betel leaves and farm products. The main purpose of this project is to provide customers with detailed information about our farming practices, fresh betel leaf products, field photos, and export services.

This website helps customers explore our farm, understand our product quality, view real field images, and easily connect with us through contact details and online communication channels.

The project focuses on creating a simple, attractive, and user-friendly online presence for an agricultural export business.
